About Us

Built on Expertise, Driven by Results

Skyline Solution Services is an Omani construction and workforce supply company established in 2024. We offer workforce supply, industrial electrical work, piping services, and all activities related to industrial construction.

We are determined to explore opportunities that require our skills and grow in the coming years.

01 Our Vision

To be one of the leading companies in Oman, emphasizing support and delivering our best services to achieve customer satisfaction and long-term sustainability.

02 Our Mission

To change how people work together offering technical know-how and hands-on guidance to support businesses, through quality, affordable, and reliable service.

What We Do

Our Services

01 Workforce Supply

Skilled and semi-skilled manpower to meet project requirements, ensuring efficiency and compliance.

02 Civil Activities

Civil construction works including foundations, structural works, concrete, and finishing.

03 Maintenance

Preventive and corrective maintenance, inspections, and emergency repairs for operational continuity.

04 Piping &Ducting

Fabrication, installation, and maintenance of piping and ducting systems for industrial facilities.

05 Electrical Works

Electrical installations, testing, and maintenance covering power distribution and industrial systems.

06 MEP Materials

Supply of mechanical, electrical, and plumbing materials sourced to specification for projects.

Reliable land transportation of goods and heavy equipment to project sites across Oman.

08 Painting &Finishing

Professional painting and finishing for industrial, commercial, and residential projects.
